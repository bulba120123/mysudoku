# Third-party data

## Sudoku Exchange Puzzle Bank

- Source: https://github.com/grantm/sudoku-exchange-puzzle-bank
- Author: Grant McLean and Sudoku Exchange contributors
- License: Public Domain

The bundled Sudoku puzzles were generated with QQWing, graded with Sukaku
Explainer, and dedicated to the public domain by the dataset publisher. The
application stores a validated subset in a compact offline format.

The Beginner and Easy levels are derived from public-domain Easy puzzles by
revealing additional solution cells. Beginner puzzles are verified as solvable
with naked singles; Easy puzzles are verified with naked and hidden singles.
The original Diabolical set is split into Expert (rating 5.0–7.4) and Master
(rating above 7.4).
